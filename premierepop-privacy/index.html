PremierePop Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

PremierePop is a browser extension for watching anime episodes together in sync and exchanging timestamped scrolling comments ("danmaku").

What data PremierePop handles

Stored only on your device:

  • Your settings (display name, danmaku appearance, spoiler/blocked word and user lists, optional party-server URL), stored in Chrome's synced storage.
  • Danmaku you write or receive, stored per episode in Chrome's local storage on your device. You can delete all of it at any time in Settings ("Delete all saved danmaku").

    • Transmitted only while you are in a watch room, only to your room:

  • Your display name, playback sync events (play/pause/seek and current timestamp), danmaku, chat messages, and emoji reactions are relayed to other members of your room so the room can stay in sync.
  • If you configure a party server (in Settings), relaying goes through that server. The reference server stores nothing: rooms exist only in memory while members are connected.
  • If no server is configured, sync uses your browser's local BroadcastChannel between your own tabs and nothing leaves your device.

    • What PremierePop does NOT do

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party scripts.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • We never access, copy, or redistribute any video or audio content from any streaming service. Each viewer uses their own subscription.

    • Contact

    Questions about this policy: use the support email listed on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
